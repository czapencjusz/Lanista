GBot Early access

A browser extension for Gladiatus

Gladiatus on autopilot

Expeditions, dungeons, the arena, healing, repairs and quests, handled from a bar inside the game.

GBot runs in your own logged-in game tab and clicks through the game the way you would: it waits for each cooldown, eats when your health runs low and takes breaks when you tell it to.

What it does

Everything you would click through yourself

Switch each activity on or off from the in-game bar and fine-tune it in the settings window.

Fights

Expeditions

Attacks the enemy you pick at your last visited location or any location from the game's list, every time the cooldown ends. It can keep some points in reserve.

Bonuses before the boss

With the boss selected, it first fights the other three enemies until their bonuses are learned, so the boss gets them too. Each win has a chance to teach one (25–34% on our test server).

Dungeons

Starts a Normal or Advanced dungeon when none is running and fights through it enemy by enemy. It never presses Cancel dungeon.

Arena and Circus Turma

Picks the lowest, highest or a random opponent in Provinciarum, stays within a level range you set, and skips players on your never-attack list or who beat you in the last 24 hours.

Enemy nests

When a win offers to search the enemy's nest, it does a quick search, a thorough search or returns to safety, as you choose.

Fight results

Reads every combat report and keeps score: wins, losses, gold, experience, honour and fame, with win rates per activity.

Upkeep

Healing

Eats food when your health drops below one threshold and stops fighting below another. It looks through every bag and picks the food that best fills the missing health.

Training

Spends gold above a reserve you set on stat points, always on the cheapest of the stats you pick. Gold you spend can't be stolen in the arena.

Workbench repair

Takes worn gear off, repairs it at the workbench with materials from your Horreum and puts it back on. Rent is paid in gold, and fights wait until the item is back. A Repair all button sits under your character.

Chores

Pantheon quests

Collects finished quests and accepts the best-paying new one of the types you allow. It skips quests for places you don't fight at and for activities that are switched off.

Stable work

Takes the job you choose for the hours you choose once your expedition and dungeon points run out.

Daily bonus and pop-ups

Collects the daily login bonus and closes notification windows so they don't get in the way.

How it plays

Paced like a person

  • Human timingA random pause before every click, 1.2 to 3.5 seconds by default.
  • No constant refreshingIt sleeps until the next cooldown ends, then looks again.
  • Hours and breaksPlay only between set hours, and take breaks of about 15 minutes every 2 hours, varied by ±30%.
  • Never spends rubiesRent, bonuses and everything else are paid with gold or earned by fighting.
  • Stops when stuckAn activity that keeps failing is paused for a while, and the log says what went wrong.
  • One tab per serverOnly one game tab runs the bot, and a tab that hangs is reloaded.

In the game

A control bar on every game page

  • Start and Stop in one click, plus a button to check the game right away.
  • A tile per activity. Click to switch it on or off. Each tile shows a live countdown, ready, no points or low HP.
  • What comes next, with your health, expedition and dungeon points and gold at a glance.
  • Drag it anywhere, minimise it, or dock it along the top of the page.
  • Settings everywhere. The same settings are in the toolbar popup and on a full options page, and you can export them to a file for another browser.

Setup

Install in about a minute

GBot isn't in the browser stores yet, so it installs as a developer extension.

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera

  1. Download the Chrome zip and unzip it into a folder you'll keep, such as Documents/GBot.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (in Edge, edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder.
  4. Open Gladiatus. The GBot bar appears on the game page. Switch on what you want and press Start.

To update, unzip the new version over the same folder and click the reload arrow on the GBot card. Your settings are kept.

Firefox

  1. Download the Firefox zip. Keep it zipped.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Click Load Temporary Add-on… and choose the zip file.
  4. Open Gladiatus. If the GBot button in the toolbar shows Grant access, click it.

Firefox removes temporary add-ons when it closes. Until there is a signed version, load it again after each restart.

If it breaks
Gameforge sometimes changes the game. GBot then pauses the activity it can't do and writes what it couldn't find in its log. Include that line when you report a problem.
Can my account get banned?

Yes. Gameforge's terms of service forbid bots. GBot plays at a human pace, but there is no guarantee. Use it at your own risk.

Does it need my password?

No. It works in the game tab you're already logged into and never logs in for you. If your session expires, log in again and it carries on.

What does it send anywhere?

Nothing. It only talks to the Gladiatus game servers, and your settings, statistics and log stay in your browser. There are no accounts and no analytics.

Does the game tab have to stay open?

Yes, but it can be in the background. The bot runs in that tab, one tab per server.

Will it spend rubies?

Never. Workbench rent is paid in gold, and expedition bonuses are only learned by winning fights.
