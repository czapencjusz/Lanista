How it works

Up and running in three steps

Install it

Download the zip for your browser and load it. It takes about a minute.

Pick what it does

A Lanista bar appears on every game page. Turn activities on with one click and fine-tune them in the settings.

Press Start

Lanista plays at a human pace, waits out the cooldowns and writes down everything it does.

Features

Everything it can do

Every feature has its own switch, so you only automate the parts you want.

In battle

{{ICON:expedition}}

Expeditions

Fights the enemy you choose every time the cooldown ends, at your last location or any one you pick. You can keep some points in reserve.

{{ICON:stats}}

Bonuses before the boss

With the boss selected, Lanista first beats the other three enemies until their bonuses are learned, so the boss gets them too.

{{ICON:dungeon}}

Dungeons

Starts a Normal or Advanced dungeon and fights through it. If you'd rather not face the boss, it skips it and starts a fresh dungeon.

{{ICON:arena}}

Arena and Circus Turma

Picks opponents by level, skips anyone on your never-attack list, avoids players who just beat you, and can go for easy wins first.

{{ICON:underworld}}

Underworld

From level 100, fights the newest enemy with the Underworld's own points, and can even enter it for you.

{{ICON:profile}}

Enemy nests

When a win lets you search the enemy's nest, Lanista does a quick or a thorough search for extra loot.

Keeping your gladiator strong

{{ICON:heal}}

Healing

Eats the food that best fills your missing health, from your bags or your packages, and stops fighting before health gets low.

{{ICON:repair}}

Repairs

Takes worn gear off, repairs it at the workbench with Horreum materials and puts it back on. Mercenaries included.

{{ICON:training}}

Training

Spends spare gold on the cheapest of the stats you pick, so they grow evenly and arena raiders can't take it.

{{ICON:auction}}

Auction house

Bids on good-value food near the end of each round, within the budget you set.

Loot and packages

{{ICON:packages}}

Resources to the Horreum

One button on the packages page moves every resource from all your packages into the Horreum.

{{ICON:general}}

Tidy packages

Every half hour it cashes in gold packages, stores resources, sells gear you don't want and saves packages that are about to expire.

{{ICON:smelting}}

Smelting

Tick items on the packages page or set rules, and Lanista keeps the smelter busy and stores what comes out.

Daily chores

{{ICON:quests}}

Pantheon quests

Hands in finished quests and takes the best-paying new one that fits what you're doing.

{{ICON:work}}

Stable work

Takes a job when your expedition and dungeon points run out.

{{ICON:refresh}}

Point refills

Uses Gate Keys and Mobilisations you already own when points run out, up to a daily limit.

{{ICON:notifications}}

Daily bonus

Collects the login bonus and closes pop-ups so nothing gets in the way.

In the game

Control it without leaving the game

A small bar sits on every game page. Start and stop the bot, switch activities on and off with one click, and see what's coming next.

  • Live status. Each tile shows a countdown, ready, no points or low HP.
  • Your layout. Drag it anywhere, minimise it, or dock it along the top of the page.
  • From the toolbar too. The browser button opens the same controls and settings.
Settings

Set it up the way you play

Every activity has its own settings: which enemy to fight, who to leave alone, how much gold to keep, when to take a break.

  • A setup per server. Two characters on two servers never get mixed up.
  • Your priorities. Choose what goes first when several things are ready at once.
  • Take it with you. Export your settings and import them in another browser.

Built to be careful

It plays it safe

Lanista is made to behave like a steady player, not a machine hammering the server.

{{ICON:safety}}Never spends rubiesRent is paid in gold, bonuses are won in fights, and buttons that cost rubies are never pressed.
{{ICON:play}}A human paceA short random pause before every click, and no constant page reloading.
{{ICON:schedule}}Your hoursPlay only between set times, with random breaks if you like.
{{ICON:log}}Stops when something's offIf an action keeps failing, Lanista pauses it and explains why in the log.
{{ICON:interface}}One tab per serverOnly one game tab runs the bot, and a frozen tab gets reloaded.

Install

Install it in about a minute

Lanista isn't in the browser stores yet, so you add it by hand. It only takes a few clicks.

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera

{{ICON:profile}}Download for ChromeVersion {{VERSION}} · {{CHROME_SIZE}}
  1. Unzip the download into a folder you'll keep, for example Documents/Lanista.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (in Edge: edge://extensions) and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder.
  4. Open Gladiatus and press Start in the Lanista bar.

Updating? Unzip the new version into the same folder and click the reload button on the Lanista card. Your settings stay as they are.

Firefox

{{ICON:profile}}Download for FirefoxVersion {{VERSION}} · {{FIREFOX_SIZE}}
  1. Keep the download as it is. There's no need to unzip it.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Click Load Temporary Add-on… and choose the zip file.
  4. Open Gladiatus. If Lanista asks for access to the site, allow it.

Firefox removes temporary add-ons when it closes, so load it again after a restart until there's a signed version.

Good to know

Can my account get banned?

Yes, it's possible. Bots break Gladiatus' rules, and Lanista can't promise it will never be noticed. Only use it if you accept that risk.

Does it need my password?

No. Lanista works in the game tab you're already logged into and never logs in for you. If your session runs out, log in again and it carries on.

Will it spend my rubies?

Never. Everything it pays for, like workbench rent or auction bids, is paid in gold.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. It only talks to the Gladiatus game servers. Your settings, statistics and log stay in your browser, and there's no account or tracking.

Can I use it on more than one server?

Yes. Each server keeps its own settings, statistics and log. Open one game tab per server.

Does the game tab have to stay open?

Yes, but it can sit in the background while you do other things.

What happens when the game changes?

Lanista pauses anything it can't do anymore and writes the reason in its log. Include that line when you report a problem, and it can usually be fixed quickly.
